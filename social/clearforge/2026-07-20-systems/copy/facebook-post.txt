Would you pay per resolved support case?
Salesforce says Agentforce Help Agent and the Customer Service Portal will be generally available in July 2026, and pay-per-resolution pricing will be available then too. That matters because support AI stops being just a feature demo and becomes an operating cost decision. If a simple case is resolved cleanly, the model can work; if it only shifts work around, the queue still exists with a different bill attached.
