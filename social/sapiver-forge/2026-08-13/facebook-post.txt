Parents using AI at work do not have spare evenings for avoidable rework.

Automating the wrong task can cost more time than doing it manually. A workflow should reduce the number of decisions you have to carry around in your head. If you only have an hour after bedtime, configuring AI for a trivial task is a poor trade. Compare the current method, the best non-AI alternative and the realistic AI benefit before you build. That is the role of the Sapiver Forge AI Opportunity Gate. Good control should reduce mental load as well as risk.
