OpenAI to terminate model access for SpaceX-owned Cursor. OpenAI has notified SpaceX that it will cut off AI model access for the Cursor coding assistant by November 12, 2026, citing a change-of-control clause and contractual trust concerns. Why it matters: This move highlights the fragility of AI infrastructure dependencies when corporate ownership changes, potentially disrupting workflows for developers relying on Cursor's multi-model platform.
