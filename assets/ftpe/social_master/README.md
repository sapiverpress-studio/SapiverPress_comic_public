# FTPE social master assets

Purpose: reusable Sapiver Press advertising material for the First-Time Sudoku Publisher Edition / FTPE campaign.

Primary product CTA: https://sapiverpress.etsy.com

Optional Commercial 900 upgrade CTA: https://sapiverpress.etsy.com?coupon=FTPE2COMMERCIAL

Rules:
- Use real FTPE product/listing assets only.
- Do not invent fake Sudoku books, fake KDP screenshots, fake covers, fake product mockups, or fake income screenshots.
- Do not place the Commercial 900 upgrade inside produced books, interiors, covers, or mock book pages.
- Commercial 900 can be mentioned only in guide/help/promo assets and campaign copy.
- Pins should be vertical 2:3, mobile-readable, and beginner-friendly.
